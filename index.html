Parents Ask Us

Common questions about life at Heriwadi

Do you offer school transport?

Yes, we have a fleet of well-maintained buses covering Ongata Rongai, Kiserian, and environs. Routes are optimized for safety and punctuality.

What creates the "Christian" environment?

We integrate biblical values into our daily routine through morning devotions, pastoral guidance, and a culture of respect and integrity.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements include a birth certificate, previous report forms (if transferring), and a brief interview with the student. Visit our Admissions page for details.
