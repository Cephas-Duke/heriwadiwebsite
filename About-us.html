Who We Are

More Than Just A School

Heriwadi Christian Schools stands as a beacon of faith-based education in Ongata Rongai.

Since 2018, we have been dedicated to providing holistic education that develops not just the mind, but the character, spirit, and leadership qualities. We believe every child is a unique creation with unlimited potential waiting to be unlocked.

We integrate Biblical principles with the CBC curriculum to ensure students grow in wisdom and stature.

Learner-centered teaching that prioritizes critical thinking, creativity, and digital literacy.

Our Mission

To encourage every learner to discover and develop their unique leadership potential while equipping them with the tools and mindset to become high achievers in a competitive world.

Our Vision

Empowering learners with a holistic approach to education, coupled with exceptional leadership skills that transform communities and nations.
